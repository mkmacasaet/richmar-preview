The Look‑Up Method

We start by doing what your customers do.

  1. 1. Look up

    On a free 30-minute call, we search for your business together, the way a new customer would. We check your website, Google, Yelp, Facebook and Instagram. You see what they see, and we tell you what to fix first. That list is yours, whether or not you hire us.

  2. 2. Build

    We create or fix the parts you choose. You check everything before it goes live.

  3. 3. Keep it current

    Every month, on the parts you choose, we post on your pages, write your articles, update your details and answer comments, messages and reviews for you. What people find stays true.

Your listings

When your Google or Yelp details are wrong, people call a dead number or drive to a locked door. When a bad review has no reply, the reviewer gets the last word.

Google and Yelp often show up before your website does. Your Google listing is the box with your hours, phone number and reviews on Google and Google Maps. Your Yelp page does the same job on Yelp.

What we set up

  • Your Google listing, created in your business name, or brought under your control if it already exists. On Standard and Pro, your Yelp page too.
  • The right name, address, phone number, hours, services and photos.
  • A link and QR code you can show customers when you ask for a Google review.

What we do every month

  • Update your details and photos whenever something changes.
  • On Standard and Pro, respond to your Google and Yelp reviews in your voice.
  • On Pro, answer your Google and Yelp messages within 24 hours, and book appointments from them.

What we can’t do

We can’t take down a bad review because you dislike it. Google and Yelp each decide what stays up, and we can report a review that breaks their rules. Yelp also asks businesses not to request reviews, so we don’t, and we won’t promise you more of them.
