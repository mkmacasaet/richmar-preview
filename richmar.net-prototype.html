Ask anything

Most independent owners do everything alone: the work, the scheduling, the invoicing and the follow-up. Marketing gets pushed to later. Customers now search Google and ask AI tools like ChatGPT who to call, so a business without a clear website is easy to miss. When someone does reach out, there’s often nobody free to answer.

  • No website, or one that’s broken

    Someone hears your name, looks you up and finds nothing, or a page that won’t load. They call the next business on the list.

  • Your social pages have gone quiet

    The last post was months ago, so people checking you out wonder if you’re still open.

  • Messages slip through the cracks

    People reach out by email, text and DM. The one you miss while you’re busy goes to whoever answers first.

  • Customers buy once and disappear

    They liked the work, but nobody stayed in touch. By the time they need you again, they’ve lost your number.

SEO and GEO, in plain English

SEO is for Google

It helps you come up when someone searches on Google. Think of it as getting a good spot in the phone book everyone uses.

SEO stands for search engine optimization.

GEO is for AI tools

It helps tools like ChatGPT learn about your business, so they can name you when someone asks. Think of it as being the business a well-connected friend recommends.

GEO stands for generative engine optimization.
